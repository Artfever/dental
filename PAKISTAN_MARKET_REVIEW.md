# Pakistan healthcare & dental web landscape — design research (14 October 2026)

## Research scope / integrity
Qualitative desk review of **16 publicly accessible Pakistani dental, hospital and diagnostic websites**, including Islamabad direct competitors and national health networks. Source material was collected from indexed public website content, not an exhaustive census of all Pakistani clinics and **not a systematic extraction of every website's rendered CSS colors**. Findings on visual positioning are interpretation; proposed hexadecimal colors are **our original design tokens**, not measured competitive palettes. Sites can change after research date. Do not describe this work as 'scraping all websites in Pakistan'.

## Large-scale medical / diagnostics websites
| Brand and live source | Observed public positioning | Design implication (our interpretation) |
|---|---|---|
| [Shifa International Hospitals](https://www.shifa.com.pk/home) | Patient portal, specialties, doctors, health library, medical network | Avoid impersonating a hospital portal or enterprise medical dashboard |
| [Maroof International Hospital](https://www.maroof.com.pk/) | Specialties, doctors, appointments, emergency access | Clinic demo should remain simple and enquiry-centered |
| [Aga Khan University Hospital Pakistan](https://hospitals.aku.edu/pakistan/home) | Broad institutional care and patient education | Trust comes from careful hierarchy, accessible information and accurate claims |
| [Chughtai Lab](https://chughtailab.com/) | Diagnostic network, laboratory reliability, patient access | Brand must signal healthcare rigor without generic lab-brand visuals |
| [Indus Hospital & Health Network](https://indushospital.org.pk/) | Hospital network, equity, access and community care | Not the right visual archetype for a single private dental practice |

## Key conclusion (design judgment, not statistical market fact)
- Two crowded storytelling lanes: big institutional healthcare ('specialists, departments, networks') and luxury dental/aesthetics ('premium, artful, signature smiles').
- Best opportunity for the Nayyar **demo**: quietly distinctive, precise, local, dentistry-first, highly readable and easy to contact.
- Recommend **deep botanical olive + porcelain + charcoal + restrained burnt terracotta**. Olive reads steady and composed without default teal; porcelain feels clean without stark hospital white; terracotta gives human warmth without the overly cosmetic blush/rose language.
- Avoid generic teal/blue gradients; also avoid leaning too hard on burgundy/rose salon luxury.

## Workflow advice
Use actual approved clinic logo, photographs, stationery and signboard as the strongest color evidence **if they are provided later**. An unverified demo palette must not be passed off as official clinic branding. See `CONTENT_POLICY.md` for factual safeguards and `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` for binding implementation rules.
