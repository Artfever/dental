If another site or tool conflicts, preserve the uncertainty and request owner verification before publication. ## Forbidden fabrications
- “Trusted by 10,000 patients”, “4.9/5 Google reviews”, “best dentist in Islamabad”, “pain-free guaranteed”, “24/7 dental emergencies”, “same-day bookings”, “free consultation” as a current offer, invented diplomas/certifications, before/after testimonials, stock imagery labeled as the practice.
- Claiming WhatsApp enquiry is a confirmed appointment.
- Claiming a second I-10 branch or linking to unrelated `nayyar-dental.com`.
- Claiming site has online records, payments, integrations, reminders, or a practice management system.

## Privacy design
- No database. Do not ask for medical symptoms, patient DOB, records, photos, prescriptions, or other sensitive health information.
- Avoid analytics, ad trackers, chat widgets, or third-party embed dependencies unless explicitly requested.
- Prefer direct `wa.me`, `tel:`, and Maps-search outbound actions. On privacy page explain that users leaving for WhatsApp/Maps are subject to those providers' terms.
- Disclose that this is a concept/demo site with clinic details pending owner sign-off.

## Publishing sign-off checklist
Confirm brand permission, exact contact numbers, address/map pin, hours, treatment menu, lead clinician information, marketing approvals, privacy disclosures, and any use of identifiable imagery before converting demo into official site.
