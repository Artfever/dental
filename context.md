## Patient-facing journey inferred from public evidence

1. **Discovery:** Patient finds the clinic via search, map/directory entries, Facebook or Instagram content. Facebook and Instagram appear to build trust with dental education and procedure examples.
2. **Enquiry:** Public Facebook posts ask people to call the landline or WhatsApp the mobile number to book; one advertises a free consultation. This is a **contact request**, not evidence of instant online scheduling.
3. **Scheduling:** A staff member likely confirms an available appointment in conversation. The exact owner of the inbox, hours of response, booking calendar, reminder procedure and triage rules are **unknown**.
4. **Visit and care:** The patient attends the I-8 clinic for assessment and agreed dental care. Actual intake forms, diagnostics, consent, treatment planning, fees, payments, records and follow-up process are **not visible publicly**.
5. **Ongoing engagement:** Public educational and promotional posts can prompt further enquiries. Automated recall, patient CRM and review requests are **not verified**.

The observable online model is **social/search discovery → direct call or WhatsApp → in-person clinic visit**. There is no verified self-service booking calendar, patient portal, online payment page, chatbot or practice management software in the reviewed public sources. Absence of public evidence does not establish that these tools are absent internally.
